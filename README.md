# 📊 Marketing Campaign Performance Analysis – NewCo (BCG X Simulation)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes a multi-channel marketing campaign conducted by **NewCo**, a national retail brand focused on driving **new customer growth**.

The campaign tested different:
- Messaging strategies (**Campaign A vs Campaign B**)  
- Marketing channels (**Email, Instagram, Web Banner**)  

The objective of this analysis was to evaluate campaign performance and provide **data-driven recommendations** on where NewCo should focus its marketing investment.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

NewCo sought to answer the following key questions:

- Which **campaign + channel combination** delivers the most value?  
- What drives **engagement and conversion** among new customers?  
- Where should marketing investment be focused to **maximize new customer growth**?

## 📈 Analysis Approach

The analysis focused on **new customer performance**, aligning with business objectives.

### Key Dimensions:
- Channel: Email, Instagram, Web Banner  
- Campaign Type: A vs B  
- Customer Type: New vs Existing  

### Key Metrics:
- **Sales (Revenue)**  
- **Conversion Rate**  
- **Time on Site (Engagement)**  

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 1. Email is the Dominant Channel
- Email outperforms all other channels in **sales, conversion, and engagement**  
- A single Email campaign nearly matches the total performance of Instagram  

---

### 2. Campaign A vs Campaign B (Trade-off)

- **Campaign A (Email):**
  - Highest new customer revenue (~$2.1k)  

- **Campaign B (Email):**
  - Highest conversion rate (**64.5%**)  

**Insight:**  
Campaign A drives revenue, while Campaign B drives conversion efficiency  

---

### 3. Engagement Does Not Guarantee Conversion

- Campaign A shows slightly higher time on site  
- Campaign B converts better despite lower engagement  

**Insight:**  
Conversion is influenced more by **message effectiveness** than time spent  

---

### 4. Web Banner Underperforms

- Low sales and conversion across all scenarios  
- Moderate engagement but poor outcomes  

**Insight:**  
Web Banner represents **inefficient marketing spend (low ROI)**

## 💡 Recommendation

NewCo should prioritize the **Email channel** using a dual-strategy approach:

- **Scale Campaign A (Email)**  
  → Maximize immediate revenue from new customers  

- **Optimize Campaign B (Email)**  
  → Leverage high conversion rates for scalable acquisition  

---

### 📌 Strategic Actions

- Reallocate budget from **Web Banner** to Email  
- Refine Campaign B messaging to improve scalability  
- Continue testing to balance **conversion vs revenue performance**  

---

## 🧠 Key Takeaways

- Email is the most effective channel for **new customer growth**  
- Campaign strategy should balance **revenue generation and conversion efficiency**  
- **Engagement alone is not a reliable predictor of conversion**  
- Data-driven insights can significantly improve marketing ROI
